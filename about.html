Gree

A privacy-first start page that brings the web back to its roots—fast, focused, and distraction-free.

Key Features

Fast

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks. Loads quickly and works offline.

Private

No tracking, no analytics, no accounts. Settings and history stay on your device.

Beautiful

Clean, distraction-free design with light, dark and system themes.

Responsive

Works on phones, tablets and desktops, and can be installed as an app.

Privacy & Legal

Gree does not collect or send any data to a server. Your theme, search engine choice and search history are stored only in your browser and can be cleared at any time from the History page.

When you search, your query is sent to the search engine you selected, and that engine's own privacy policy applies.
